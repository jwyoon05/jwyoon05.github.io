I am an undergraduate student in the [School of Biological Sciences](https://biosci.snu.ac.kr/) at Seoul National University. I am also an undergraduate intern in the [Steinegger Lab](https://steineggerlab.com/en/), advised by Prof. Dr. Martin Steinegger.  

My research interest lies in developing integrated computational pipelines to solve problems regarding the sequence-structure-function paradigm in scale. Specific examples would include developing computational tools applicable to predict in vivo biomolecular interactions and designing molecular binders capable to be used in disease treatment. My past research experience includes improving Folddisco, an open-source software that can search structure motifs in scale.

I am passionate about developing softwares and DL models applicable in researches in the field of computational biology. See my [GitHub](https://github.com/jwyoon05) for more.
